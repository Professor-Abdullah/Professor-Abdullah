## 🏆 `Team Project`

### 🗣️ **BolKhata — Voice-First Digital Ledger**

**Team Trojan · Alibaba Cloud AI Hackathon Pakistan 2026**

A voice-first digital ledger designed for small shopkeepers. The system lets a shopkeeper speak a transaction, converts speech to text, extracts transaction intent with an LLM, generates a spoken confirmation, and records the transaction in the digital khata.

**My role:** Team member / contributor.  
This was a collaborative Team Trojan project; I do not claim sole ownership of the implementation.

**Stack used by the project:**

`React Native` · `Expo` · `TypeScript` · `Express` · `Supabase` · `Clerk`
